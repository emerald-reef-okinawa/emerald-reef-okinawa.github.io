Why do people from Taiwan love Okinawa? The closest island resort to Taiwan

Okinawa is about 1.5 hours by direct flight from Taipei or Kaohsiung — far closer than Guam or Cebu, with no visa needed and just a 1-hour time difference, so it works even for a short long-weekend trip. That ease is why travelers from Taiwan keep choosing it. The real draw is the emerald sea with 30m visibility: the Blue Cave, sea turtles, and colourful tropical fish. You also get Japanese streets, food and shopping all in one trip — outstanding value.

When is the best season for the ocean in Okinawa? A guide for travelers from Taiwan

The best window is April to November, when warm water lets you swim comfortably without a wetsuit. Since Taiwan shares a similar climate, the heat will feel familiar. The late June–July period after the rainy season and the peak-visibility months of October–November are when the sea is most beautiful. Holidays (Lunar New Year, Tomb-Sweeping, National Day, summer) fill flights and hotels, so book 2–3 months ahead. During typhoon season (Aug–Sep), pick a tour with flexible cancellation.

Top 3 marine activities travelers from Taiwan must try in Okinawa

① Blue Cave snorkeling — shallow water and a life jacket mean non-swimmers are fine, and the mystical blue glow is hugely photogenic; great for families, couples and friends. ② Sea turtle snorkeling — a once-in-a-lifetime swim with wild green turtles, where a local guide who knows the high-encounter spots is key. ③ Skin diving (freediving) — minimal diving on a single breath that lets you get close to fish and turtles for dramatic photos. All are fine for beginners and kids and take half a day.

No Japanese needed — booking in Chinese, language, payment, getting around

"Can I book without Japanese?" Don't worry. Emerald Reef handles everything by message, and consultations in Chinese are possible via translation. Book anytime via LINE or the contact form. On the day we provide free hotel pickup, so no tricky transport or Japanese directions are needed — you can join even without a rental car. Many shops accept cash or card on site. In the water, simple English or Chinese plus gestures are plenty.
