For Travelers from Taiwan: Why Choose a Fully Private Tour? The Peace of Mind of Never Sharing Your Boat with Strangers

Published：2026.09.03
日本語繁體中文한국어

On a shared tour, you end up worrying about a family member who can't swim well, and strangers keep photobombing your shots. Every Emerald Reef booking is a fully private charter, so none of that happens. Here's why a private tour brings real peace of mind, written for travelers from Taiwan.

That subtle unease on a shared tour — do you know it?

Most Okinawa snorkel tours are "shared tours" — several bookings combined onto the same boat with the same group. The more people, the more each guide has to watch, and it gets tiring when your party includes a nervous swimmer, a small child, or an older family member. You have to match a stranger's pace, and strangers end up in your photos too. This is your Okinawa trip — you deserve to enjoy it without that background worry.

On a private charter, the guide watches only your family

Every Emerald Reef tour is fully private. Each departure carries only the group that booked it. The guide stays right beside you — teaching a nervous swimmer how to float, holding a child's hand — with no stranger's pace to match. Rest when you want, take your time with photos when you want, and never share the water with people you've never met.

Swimming levels differ even within one family — here's why that's fine

Many guests from Taiwan travel with family or friends, and it's common for one parent to swim well while the other and the kids can't. Because the tour is private, we can match each person's pace even within one group. Non-swimmers enjoy plenty just floating in a life jacket, while confident swimmers can head a little further out. Nobody has to move at the same speed, so the gap in swimming ability within your own family stops being something to worry about.

Worried whether Mandarin or Taiwanese Mandarin will work?

Bookings and questions are accepted 24/7 via LINE or the contact form, and consultations in Chinese (Traditional) via translation are welcome. On the day, we explain everything with photos and gestures too, so not speaking Japanese is never a problem.

About Emerald Reef's fully private tours

Free pickup from hotels in the Onna Village area, with mask, fins, and life jacket all free to rent. We shoot with a two-camera setup — the guide's and yours — and every photo is emailed to you free after the tour. Snorkeling starts at one person, with two-person groups from ¥7,000 per person. Blue Cave, the Naha sea-turtle tour, and skin diving are all private charter too. No more watching yourself on a shared tour — just your family, and Okinawa's ocean.

A private tour, just your group

Free hotel transfer and free photos. Ages 3 and up. Same-day and next-day bookings are welcome when we have space.
