Snorkeling from Naha: Complete Guide — Top 5 Spots & How to Choose a Pickup Tour

Published：2026.06.04
日本語繁體中文한국어

Everything you need to snorkel from Naha — nearby spots, Kerama Islands, Blue Cave, and how to pick the right pickup tour.

Use Naha as your base and explore Okinawa's ocean to the fullest.

"Can you snorkel from Naha?" — we hear this constantly. The answer is yes. Naha itself doesn't have prime snorkeling beaches, but with pickup-included tours you can reach stunning spots in 30–60 minutes. This guide covers every spot reachable from Naha, how to pick a tour, and a model 2-day itinerary.

Is there snorkeling in Naha city itself? An honest answer

Naha's coastline (including Naminoue Beach) is not suited for snorkeling due to water quality and limited visibility near the busy port. But this is no reason to give up. Naha is Okinawa's main transport hub, with easy access north and south — Japan's best snorkeling spots are within 30–60 minutes. The Kerama Islands are just 50 minutes by ferry from Tomari Port. Choose a pickup-included tour and every spot is reachable without a rental car.

① About 30 minutes from Naha — Senaga Island & Nanjo area

About 10 minutes from Naha Airport by car, Senaga Island is a resort island famous for Umikaji Terrace. Its shallow, gentle waters offer casual snorkeling with tropical fish near the rocky areas. Around 30 minutes south of Naha, Nanjo City's Okino-shima area is a local favorite with coral and tropical fish. Neither site matches Blue Cave or Kerama in visibility or variety, but they are the easiest sea access from Naha and easy to fit into any itinerary.

② About 20 minutes from Naha — John Man Beach (Itoman City)

About 20–25 minutes south of Naha along Route 331, John Man Beach sits along the Itoman coastline. Named after Nakahama Manjiro — the Edo-period fisherman nicknamed "John" who was rescued by an American whaling crew — this beach is a local favorite away from the tourist crowds. Shallow, calm water makes it great for beginners and families, while rocky areas host tropical fish and coral. Perfect for a quick ocean dip between sightseeing stops without the long drive.

③ About 50 minutes from Naha (ferry) — Kerama Islands "Kerama Blue"

From Tomari Port in Naha, a 50-minute high-speed ferry brings you to the Kerama Islands — world-famous for "Kerama Blue" clarity exceeding 30m. Designated a national park in 2014, the islands host approximately 10% of all coral species documented worldwide. Zamami, Aka, and Tokashiki islands all have multiple calm snorkeling points, and the area is renowned for exceptionally high sea turtle encounter rates. Your dream of swimming with a sea turtle can come true on a half-day trip from Naha.

④ About 1 hour from Naha (by car) — Blue Cave (Onna Village, Maeda Cape)

About 50–60 minutes north of Naha along Route 58, Blue Cave at Maeda Cape in Onna Village is Okinawa's most iconic snorkeling destination. Sunlight refracts through a submerged rock gap and turns the cave a mystical blue — Okinawa's most photographed underwater sight. At 1–5m depth it's ideal for beginners, with 100+ species including clownfish and butterfly fish. With a pickup-included tour from Naha, you can leave in the morning and return by early afternoon. The light peaks on sunny days between 10am and noon.

4 key points for choosing a snorkeling tour from Naha

① Hotel pickup from Naha included: Spots are 30–60 minutes away. For travelers without a rental car, pickup is essential. Confirm the coverage area (Naha and nearby, etc.) in advance. ② Private charter vs group tour: Large group tours are hard for a guide to manage well and leave little room for your own pace. Private charter gives you the ocean to yourselves. ③ All equipment included: Renting mask, fins, and life jacket separately adds up quickly. Choose a tour where all gear is included at no extra charge. ④ Photo/video service: Capturing underwater moments on your own is difficult. A service where the guide takes underwater photos and gives you your photos free is invaluable for preserving memories.
