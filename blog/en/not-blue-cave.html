You Don’t Have to Go to the Blue Cave — If You Just Want to Swim With Fish, There Are Better Reefs

Published：2026.10.02
日本語繁體中文한국어

Search for snorkeling in Okinawa and the Blue Cave comes up first. But it is popular enough to get crowded, rough days close it, and inside the cave the fish are not really the point. If what you want is to swim with fish, there is another option.

On a rough day you simply cannot go in

The Blue Cave sits in the rocks off Cape Maeda, and when a swell runs it is unsafe and the tour is called off. That is not a question of which operator you book; the sea decides. Our own reviews say as much more than once: "High waves meant we could not reach the Blue Cave, but we were still completely satisfied." Losing a highlight of your Okinawa trip to the forecast is a shame. Not building the day around the cave in the first place removes that worry entirely.

Inside the cave, fish are not the attraction

This gets misread a lot: the Blue Cave is about light, not fish. Sunlight entering below the surface bounces off the sand and fills the cavern with blue — genuinely beautiful. But it is dark in there, and both the coral and the fish are far richer out on the open reef. The feeding happens outside the cave too. In other words, if your goal is to be surrounded by colourful fish, the time spent inside the cave is not spent on that goal. Neither is better; they are simply different things.

We can simply go wherever the sea is best that day

Our Onna snorkeling is not tied to one fixed destination. We look at the wind, the swell and the crowds on the day, and take you to whichever point is calmest and busiest with fish. On days when everyone has piled into the famous spot, a quiet reef a little further along is often empty. Transfers are included, so the driving is our problem, not yours. It also costs less — from ¥6,000 per person for groups of three or more, against ¥7,000 for the Blue Cave. The fish feeding, the free photo and video data, the one-group-only format and the private shower and changing tent are identical on both plans.
