Okinawa Blue Cave Snorkeling: The Complete 2026 Guide (Best Season, What to Bring, Beginner Tips)

Published：2026.06.16

The Blue Cave in Onna Village is Okinawa's most popular snorkel spot. A local guide explains why it glows blue, the best time to go, what to bring, and how to ease first-timer nerves.

What is Okinawa's Blue Cave, and why does it glow blue?

The Blue Cave is a sea cave right next to Maeda Cape in Onna Village. Sunlight enters the cave, reflects off the white sandy seabed, and as it passes back through the water the longer red wavelengths are absorbed, leaving only the short blue wavelengths — so the whole surface glows an unreal blue. It is the same principle as the famous Blue Grotto on Capri, Italy, but in Okinawa you can experience it in shallow, beginner-friendly water.

Best season and time of day to see the Blue Cave at its bluest

The Blue Cave can be enjoyed year-round, but April to November offers the calmest, clearest water — comfortable even without a wetsuit. The cave glows bluest on sunny days between 10am and 1pm, when the high sun reaches deep inside. Early morning, evening, and cloudy days look less vivid. To avoid crowds, book an early-morning slot. A local guide reads the day's weather, tide, and sun position to take you at the most beautiful moment.

Can't swim or traveling with kids? Easing the most common worries

"I can't swim — is that OK?" Yes. With a life jacket you float naturally, so swimming ability isn't needed and a guide stays beside you. "What age can kids join?" Snorkeling is possible from around age 4, starting with fish-watching in the shallows. "Will my ears hurt?" Since you don't dive deep, ear equalizing is rarely needed. "What if water gets in my mask?" We teach you exactly what to do before entering. Our tours are fully private, so you go at your own pace.

Boat entry vs beach entry — which should you choose?

There are two ways to reach the Blue Cave. Beach entry uses the stairs at Maeda Cape — affordable, but it requires climbing steps and a 5–10 minute swim to the cave. Boat entry takes you by boat close to the cave — ideal for those less confident in their fitness, families with small children, or weaker swimmers, with much less walking. For first-timers and families we recommend boat entry or a small-group private beach entry. We suggest the best option based on your fitness and wishes.
