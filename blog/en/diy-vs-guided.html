Can’t You Just Snorkel Off the Beach for Free? What a Guided Tour Actually Changes

Published：2026.10.02
日本語繁體中文한국어

Straight answer first: yes, you can snorkel off an Okinawan beach by yourself, and some beaches are free. So what does paying for a guide actually buy you? Here is the honest version, from the people who do it for a living.

You cannot tell where the fish are just by looking

From the shore, every stretch of sea looks equally lovely. But underwater they are different worlds, and plenty of areas are nothing but sand going on and on. Fish gather on coral heads, at breaks in the reef, where the current hits the terrain — and almost none of that is visible from the surface. A lot of "I swam in Okinawa but there were fewer fish than I expected" comes down to where people swam. We read the tide and the wind that day and take you straight to where the fish are.

The sea is sometimes more dangerous for people who can swim

Okinawa has rip currents. Swimming towards shore and not moving is a situation strong swimmers handle worst, because they decide they can power through and burn their reserves doing it. Heading offshore without knowing the terrain or the state of the tide is the most dangerous way to do this. We run one group per tour with your guide beside you the whole time. Everyone gets a life jacket, and we start in shallow water where you can stand. That is a different proposition from a family heading out alone off an unsupervised free beach.

The one thing you cannot take yourself is a photo of yourselves

You can rent gear from a shop on the island. But the one thing you cannot get for yourselves, by any route, is a photo of your group underwater. Your guide films on an Insta360 and lends you a second one, so you come away with your family’s faces and the view from your own eyes. There is no limit on the number of shots and the data is free. At ¥6,000 per person for three or more, that price covers transfers, all the gear, the fish feeding, the filming, and a private shower and changing tent. Once you add up rental, parking and the time it takes, the gap is smaller than it looks.
