Your Boat Tour Got Cancelled? How to Find a Same-Day Backup for the Sea in Okinawa

Published：2026.09.22
日本語繁體中文한국어

Wind or swell cancels your boat tour — the Kerama trip, the glass-bottom boat — and suddenly your day is empty. It happens a lot in Okinawa in September and October. But a beach-entry snorkel that does not rely on a boat can sometimes still run, by choosing a spot sheltered from the wind. Here is how to find a same-day backup.

Why boat tours get cancelled so often

Boat tours to the Kerama Islands and glass-bottom boats cross open water or head offshore, so wind and swell hit them hard. When a port or operator decides not to sail, the tour is off even if the water itself is not that rough. In September, at the tail of typhoon season, and in October as the north wind starts, it is not unusual to get a "no sailing today" message that morning. If a whole day of your trip suddenly opens up, it helps to know about the alternative: snorkeling that does not need a boat.

Beach-entry snorkeling can pick a spot by wind direction

Emerald Reef tours enter the water straight from the beach — no boat involved. On Okinawa's main island the wind can hit the east and west coasts very differently, so we check the day's wind direction and pick a calm spot on the sheltered side. One guest wrote: "It was windy and whether we could go was borderline, but they picked a safe spot to match everyone's swimming level." Of course, if every spot is beyond our safety limits, we cancel. We never push it — but it is worth knowing that there are days when the boats cannot go and the beach still can.

A real example: a family whose glass-bottom boat was cancelled

In August we met a family who wrote: "We arrived in Okinawa along with a typhoon. Our glass-boat trip was cancelled, so we decided to try snorkeling and looked for somewhere our child could join — and found Emerald Reef." Their 4-year-old daughter cried hard at first, but with a fully private tour and no one else around she settled in at her own pace, and by the end was smiling and saying "I want to do more!" They told us: "We thought the typhoon had ruined everything, but we made a genuinely happy memory." Because we take children from age 3, it works as a backup even with little ones, on the same day the cancellation comes in.

When the cancellation comes, message us right away

When the cancellation message arrives, get in touch on LINE or through the contact form as soon as you can. Include the number of people, children's ages, your hotel and whether everyone can swim, and we will check that day's conditions and availability and reply right away. We welcome same-day and next-day bookings, pick you up at your hotel, lend mask, fins and life jacket free, and give you the photo data free (video free for a limited time too). We cancel too if conditions exceed our limits, and then offer a new date, or a full refund if that will not work. We would love to help turn a day that fell apart into one you are glad you came.
