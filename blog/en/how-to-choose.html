How to Choose a Snorkeling Tour in Okinawa: 7 Checkpoints So First-Timers Do Not Regret It

Published：2026.09.22
日本語繁體中文한국어

Search for snorkeling tours in Okinawa and you get a wall of options. Choose on price alone and you may regret it: no pickup, photos that cost extra, a day spent keeping pace with strangers. A working snorkel guide lays out the seven things that actually matter when you choose.

① Private or shared ② Pickup, and which areas it covers

First check ① private or shared. Shared tours can be cheaper, but you move at a group pace with strangers, and it gets awkward if someone in your party is a nervous swimmer. On a private tour the guide's attention is on your group alone. Next, ② is there pickup, and is your hotel in the area? Meet-at-the-beach tours sound simple, but driving a rental car, finding parking and heading home in a wet swimsuit add up. Even with pickup, coverage differs from shop to shop, so check by the name of your hotel's area. Every Emerald Reef tour is fully private, with pickup from hotels in central Okinawa (Onna, Yomitan, Chatan, Okinawa City and more) and in Naha — the area depends on the plan.

③ What the price includes ④ How photos and video are handled

When comparing prices, always check ③ what the listed price actually includes. Gear rental, pickup, fish feeding or showers can be extras, and the total ends up higher. Another point where tours differ is ④ photos and video — some charge for the data or require you to buy an SD card. One guest who chose us wrote: "What sold us: pickup, one group only, filming by both the guide and us, and getting the video and photos — not having to buy an SD card was key." With Emerald Reef, pickup, mask/fins/life jacket, fish feeding and photo data are all included, and video data is free for a limited time.

⑤ Safety: qualifications, insurance, registration ⑥ Cancellation decisions and policy

In any ocean activity, safety comes first. ⑤ Check the guide's qualifications, insurance and official registration on the shop's site or booking page. Our guide holds PADI Snorkel Guide and EFR (Emergency First Response) certifications, we carry liability insurance, and we are registered with the Okinawa Prefectural Public Safety Commission. Also look at ⑥ how cancellations are decided and what the policy is: does the shop state plainly that it will not go out when conditions are dangerous, and is there a reschedule or refund for bad weather? We cancel whenever conditions exceed our safety limits, and offer a new date, or a full refund if that will not work.

⑦ Read what reviews say, not just how many there are

Finally, ⑦ reviews. Do not stop at the star rating or the count — look for people like you. Non-swimmers, families with small kids, older travellers, solo guests: how someone with the same worries as you felt is the best evidence there is. Emerald Reef has 34 reviews across Activity Japan, Jalan, asoview, GetYourGuide and Google Maps, all rated 5.0 (as of September 2026). Among them: "It really helped, as my son is not a strong swimmer," and the 4-year-old who joined on a typhoon day and ended up smiling and asking to do more. Adult prices start from ¥6,000 per person for snorkeling in Onna, ¥7,000 for the Blue Cave and ¥10,000 for Naha sea turtles. Compare all seven points — we hope you will choose us.
