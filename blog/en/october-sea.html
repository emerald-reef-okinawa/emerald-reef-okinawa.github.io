Can You Still Swim in Okinawa in October? Why It May Be the Smartest Month — Water Temps, What to Wear, Empty Water

Published：2026.09.10
日本語繁體中文한국어

"Is the swimming season over in Okinawa by October?" Not at all. Water typically holds around 26–27°C, typhoons thin out, and the tourist peak has passed — so just as the mainland reaches for autumn clothes, Okinawa's ocean enters its calmest, most relaxed stretch of the year.

Still 26–27°C: summer water carries on

The mainland is switching to autumn clothes, but Okinawa's sea is still in summer through October. Water typically sits around 26–27°C — only a little below the August–September peak. That is why most beaches here keep their swimming season open until the end of October. A swimsuit and life jacket are all you need, though a rash guard is worth packing if you feel the cold. We provide mask, fins and life jacket free, but we do not stock wetsuits, so talk to us in advance if you plan long sessions in late October.

Fewer typhoons, steadier seas

From October, typhoon approaches drop off noticeably. You spend far fewer days staring at the forecast than in September, and the odds of running as planned go up — the single biggest advantage of the month. Steadier conditions also mean longer runs of high-visibility days, and at a spot like the Blue Cave, where the whole appeal is how light enters the water, that difference is obvious. If your travel dates cannot move, or you simply want to be sure you get in the water, we sometimes recommend October over September.

The peak has passed — the water and the hotels are empty

By October both the summer holidays and Silver Week are over and the tourist peak has passed. You rarely overlap with another group even at popular spots, and you get stretches where the sea feels like yours alone. Flights and hotels calm down from summer prices too, so the same budget can buy a better trip. Emerald Reef is fully private, which pairs perfectly with an empty ocean. With fewer people around the fish are settled, so the feeding experience gives you time to really watch them. It is also the easiest season for clean photos with no strangers in the background.

What to wear in October, and how to book

Packing is much the same as summer: swimsuit, towel, a change of clothes. What October adds is a cooler breeze once you are out of the water, so a layer to throw on and a rash guard make the day more comfortable. The sun is gentler than midsummer but UV is still strong, so do not skip sunscreen — reef-safe if possible. The slot to aim for is the morning: wind is lighter, the sea is calmer, and the Blue Cave's blue looks its best between about 10am and noon.
